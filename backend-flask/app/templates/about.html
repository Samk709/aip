About Neuroguard AI

Neuroguard AI is an advanced, privacy-first Mental Health AI Intelligence system designed to predict, detect, and escalate mental distress safely. Through multidimensional risk scoring, the system synthesizes NLP text analysis, audio stress markers, and biometric facial analysis into a single Mental Health Safety Index (MHSI).

Utilizing large language models configured for empathetic memory and Explainable AI (XAI) transparency, Neuroguard AI provides real-time digital therapeutics while keeping clinicians informed of impending crisis states via the proprietary Emotion Drift Detection algorithm.
