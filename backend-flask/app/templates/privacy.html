Privacy Policy & Edge Architecture

Data privacy in mental health is non-negotiable. Neuroguard AI operates on a primary edge-compute architecture.

Facial expression and vocal tone analysis are conducted natively within the browser using WebGL and WebAudio APIs. Raw media files never touch our servers. System interactions are encrypted and the platform supports fully anonymous guest sessions for crisis triaging without identity disclosure.
